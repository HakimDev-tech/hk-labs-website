# HK Labs — written outreach operating guide

## Target segments
1. Small web agencies and independent designers who may need reliable frontend implementation support.
2. Small businesses with an observable, specific website issue.

Prioritize prospects with a real website, recent activity, a visible improvement opportunity and a relevant contact channel.

## Qualification checklist
- Is the business active?
- Is the website owned by the prospect and publicly accessible?
- Can one concrete issue be identified?
- Is the issue within the current technical scope?
- Is there a relevant business contact channel?
- Is the prospect in a jurisdiction where the chosen outreach channel is permitted?

A publicly visible email address is not automatic consent. Check local rules before commercial outreach. Identify yourself accurately, keep messages relevant and honor opt-outs immediately.

## First message structure
1. Short context showing why this business was selected.
2. One specific, verifiable observation about its website.
3. One practical improvement you could make.
4. A low-friction question asking whether a short written breakdown would be useful.

Avoid generic praise, fake urgency, fabricated results and unsolicited large attachments.

## CRM stages
New → Qualified → Contacted → Replied → Scope requested → Proposal sent → Won / Lost / Do not contact.

Record the evidence behind each lead score. Stop contacting anyone who opts out.
