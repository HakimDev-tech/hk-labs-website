# Portfolio demonstration briefs

All projects below are self-initiated concepts. They are not client engagements and must be labelled accordingly.

## 1. Northline Construction — business website concept
**Goal:** Demonstrate a trustworthy local-service business website.
Required sections: hero with one clear CTA, services, selected projects, process, trust signals, FAQ and written contact path.
Quality criteria: responsive from 360px to wide desktop, accessible contrast, semantic headings, clear service hierarchy, optimized imagery and metadata.

## 2. Orbit — SaaS landing page concept
**Goal:** Demonstrate product storytelling and conversion-focused structure.
Required sections: product promise, workflow visualization, benefits, feature details, pricing concept, FAQ and CTA.
Quality criteria: concise copy, no unsupported performance claims, clear information hierarchy, mobile-first behavior and reusable components.

## 3. Frontend Rescue — technical demo
**Goal:** Show practical frontend engineering ability.
Create a before/after case with intentionally flawed spacing, overflow, contrast and responsive behavior, then document the fixes. Include keyboard navigation and semantic markup checks.
